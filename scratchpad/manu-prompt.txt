$imagegen
Use case: illustration-story

ASSET
One square image, 2304x2304. A clean 3x3 grid of nine equal square panels
(each 768x768), separated by thin uniform white gutters about 1% of the
image width. No outer border, no drop shadow between panels, no page edge.

SCENE TO ILLUSTRATE (same scene restaged nine ways)
The mnemonic sentence: "A blind man reads you Braille with trembling hands."
An older blind man with closed or clouded eyes and small round dark
glasses sits close to a younger person (you) and reads aloud from an open
Braille book - a thick page covered in raised bump dots. His bare hands
tremble visibly (small motion lines, a slightly shaky contour) as his
fingertips sweep across the rows of Braille dots. The younger person leans
in and listens. Every concrete noun must be visible in every panel: the
blind man, his dark glasses / closed eyes, the younger listening person
(you), the open Braille book with rows of raised dots, and his two
trembling hands on the page.

THE MNEMONIC HOOK ELEMENT
The blind man's two trembling hands on the Braille page. In every panel
the hands get the compositional emphasis: foreground placement, the
boldest and most confident brush-pen contour on the sheet (with a faint
shake/doubled line to read as trembling), and the most saturated gouache
block on the sheet - a warm ochre/tan skin tone that is the single most
saturated colour anywhere on the sheet. The hands and fingertips on the
dots must be the first thing the eye lands on in every panel.

HOUSE STYLE (identical in all nine panels - one artist, nine sketches)
Brush-pen comic line art with light gouache colour blocks. Hand-drawn
INDIA-INK BRUSH linework with STRONG line-weight variation: thick, bold,
visibly tapered outer contours that swell and thin along their length,
against much thinner scratchy interior detail lines. Include a little
looseness and the occasional broken or skipped stroke where the brush
lifts. This must read as hand-brushed, NOT as a smooth uniform digital
outline. NEVER a clean even-weight vector contour, never a soft rounded
storybook outline of constant width.
Expressive, slightly exaggerated comic faces and poses. Over the line art,
soft flat gouache colour blocks: slightly chalky, low-saturation,
naturalistic fills across the main shapes. At most 3 flat value steps per
shape, hard edges, one flat colour per area. The trembling hands carry the
single most saturated block and the boldest contour.

OBJECT-LEVEL NEGATIVES (hard constraints)
No crosshatching, no hatching lines, no stippling, no pencil texture, no
engraving texture, no paper grain, no aged-paper texture. No individual
hair strands, no fabric weave, no wood-grain lines, no rendered patterns -
clothing, chairs, walls, book cover are each one flat colour with at most
a few simple flat shapes on top. The Braille dots are small evenly spaced
flat circles, not a rendered embossed texture. No volumetric or rendered
shading, no soft gradients, no glossy highlights, no drop shadows, no wash
bleed. Do not outline every fill. Backgrounds sparse: a couple of flat
shapes (a window, a doorway, a plain chair) and generous white space,
never a fully furnished textured room.

PANEL 1 IS THE STYLE KEY
Panel 1 (top-left): eye-level medium two-shot. The blind man sits on the
left in small round dark glasses, the open Braille book on his lap, both
hands flat on the page mid-sweep with faint shake lines. The younger
person sits on the right leaning in to listen. Plain flat wall behind,
one simple window shape, lots of white space. Draw its contours with a
thick tapered brush line that clearly varies in width, with one or two
broken strokes. Render this panel first; the other eight panels must
match its brush line weight and taper, its flat-fill treatment, and its
limited low-saturation palette exactly.

No volumetric face or fabric modelling, no soft grey shading on skin or
clothing - keep every fill flat.

THE NINE STAGINGS (row-major; same blind man, same younger listener, same
Braille book, same trembling hands, same story beat - only the staging
changes)
1. Eye-level medium two-shot: blind man left with dark glasses and Braille
   book on his lap, both hands sweeping the page, younger person right
   leaning in to listen.
2. Tight close-up: the two trembling hands and fingertips fill the frame,
   moving across three rows of raised Braille dots; the man's chin and
   dark glasses just visible at the top edge.
3. Low hero angle looking up: the blind man sits tall holding the open
   book up near his chest, hands trembling on the page, the younger person
   small in the lower foreground looking up and listening.
4. Wide establishing shot: a bare room, the two figures on a plain bench
   at the far side, the open Braille book between them, huge empty room
   and white space, the man's hands still the visual anchor.
5. Over-the-shoulder from behind the younger person: we look past their
   shoulder at the blind man's face and his two shaking hands on the open
   Braille page.
6. Top-down view: the open Braille book seen from directly above, rows of
   raised dots, the blind man's two trembling hands spread across it from
   one edge, the younger person's hand resting at the opposite edge.
7. Side profile: blind man in profile on the left, mouth open reading
   aloud, one hand trembling on the page and one raised slightly off it;
   younger person in profile on the right, eyes closed, listening.
8. Three-quarter view, close: the younger person gently steadies the blind
   man's shaking wrist with one hand while his fingertips stay on the
   Braille dots, both faces calm and focused.
9. After: the blind man lowers his trembling hands into his lap, the
   Braille book closed on his knee, a faint tired smile; the younger
   person claps softly or nods. Plain wall, window shape behind.

TEXT
No panel labels, numbers, captions, watermarks, signatures, or title
text. No speech bubbles required. If any lettering appears on the book
spine keep it short and correctly spelled or leave it blank.

INPUT IMAGES
Image 1: style reference for line looseness, line-weight variation, flat
limited low-saturation palette, and sparse flat backgrounds with white
space ONLY - do NOT copy its content, characters, or composition, and do
NOT copy its crosshatch shading, fur modelling, gradients, or rendered
background objects; the object-level negatives in this brief override
anything the reference does.

AVOID
watermark, signature, artist logo, extra borders, drop shadows between
panels, photorealistic real people's faces, gore, paragraphs of text,
garbled lettering.
