$imagegen
Use case: illustration-story

ASSET
One square image, 2304x2304. A clean 3x3 grid of nine equal square panels
(each 768x768), separated by thin uniform white gutters about 1% of the
image width. No outer border, no drop shadow between panels, no page edge.

SCENE TO ILLUSTRATE (same scene restaged nine ways)
The mnemonic sentence: "One pea a day, Nia lacks food." A thin young girl
named Nia sits at a nearly bare wooden table. On the table is a single
round green pea on an otherwise empty plate. That one pea is her whole
meal for the day. Her cupboard and bowls are empty; she looks hungry and
weak. Every concrete noun must be visible in every panel: Nia, the one
green pea, the empty plate, the bare table, an empty cupboard or empty
bowl showing the lack of food.

THE MNEMONIC HOOK ELEMENT
The single green pea. In every panel the pea gets the compositional
emphasis: foreground placement, the boldest and most confident brush-pen
contour on the sheet, and the most saturated gouache block on the sheet
(a clear mid-green). It is tiny but it must be the first thing the eye
lands on in every panel - oversized visual weight relative to its real
size, e.g. Nia staring right at it, pointing at it, holding it up.

HOUSE STYLE (identical in all nine panels - one artist, nine sketches)
Brush-pen comic line art with light gouache colour blocks. Hand-drawn
brush-pen linework with clear line-weight variation: bold, confident
outer contours, much thinner interior detail lines, a little looseness
and the occasional broken line. Never a clean even-weight vector contour.
Expressive, slightly exaggerated comic faces and poses. Over the line art,
soft flat gouache colour blocks: slightly chalky, low-saturation,
naturalistic fills across the main shapes. At most 3 flat value steps per
shape, hard edges, one flat colour per area. The green pea carries the
single most saturated block and the boldest contour.

OBJECT-LEVEL NEGATIVES (hard constraints)
No crosshatching, no hatching lines, no stippling, no pencil texture, no
engraving texture, no paper grain, no aged-paper texture. No individual
wood-grain lines, no fabric weave, no rendered patterns - wallpaper,
tablecloth, rug, clothing are each one flat colour with at most a few
simple flat shapes on top. No volumetric or rendered shading, no soft
gradients, no glossy highlights, no drop shadows, no wash bleed. Do not
outline every fill. Backgrounds sparse: a couple of flat shapes (a
window, a doorway, an empty shelf) and generous white space, never a
fully furnished textured room.

PANEL 1 IS THE STYLE KEY
Panel 1 (top-left): eye-level medium shot. Nia sits square at the bare
table, both hands flat on the wood, staring down at the single green pea
on the white plate in front of her. Empty cupboard visible behind as two
flat rectangles with bare shelves. Flat cream wall, lots of white space.
Render this panel first; the other eight panels must match its line
weight, its flat-fill treatment, and its limited low-saturation palette
exactly.

THE NINE STAGINGS (row-major; same girl, same pea, same plate, same bare
table, same story beat - only the staging changes)
1. Eye-level medium shot: Nia sits square at the table, hands flat on the
   wood, staring down at the single pea on the white plate. Empty cupboard
   behind.
2. Tight close-up: the single green pea on the white plate fills the
   lower foreground, huge and sharp; Nia's hungry face is soft and
   blurred small behind it.
3. Low hero angle looking up: Nia holds the one pea pinched between finger
   and thumb above her head, squinting at it against a pale window.
4. Wide establishing shot: the whole bare kitchen - Nia tiny at the far
   table, one pea on the plate, empty open cupboard, empty bowl on the
   floor, huge empty room, lots of white space.
5. Over-the-shoulder from behind Nia: we look past her thin shoulder and
   messy hair down at the plate with its one green pea and nothing else.
6. Top-down flat lay: the round table seen from directly above - white
   plate dead centre, single green pea on it, an empty bowl and an
   upturned empty cup beside it, Nia's two hands entering from the bottom
   edge.
7. Side profile, seated: Nia in profile lifts a fork with the single pea
   speared on the very tip toward her open mouth, eyes wide, ribs showing
   through her shirt.
8. Three-quarter view, standing: Nia stands on tiptoe pulling open a bare
   cupboard with one hand, other hand cupping the single pea protectively
   at her chest, dismayed expression.
9. After the meal: the empty white plate with a tiny green smear where the
   pea was, fork set down; Nia slumped back in the chair, one hand on her
   hollow stomach, a wall calendar behind marked with a small green dot on
   one day.

TEXT
No panel labels, numbers, captions, watermarks, signatures, or title
text. Diegetic text only if needed: the wall calendar in panel 9 may show
a short month name or a single green dot - keep any lettering short and
correctly spelled. No speech bubbles required.

INPUT IMAGES
Image 1: style reference for line looseness, line-weight variation, flat
limited low-saturation palette, and sparse flat backgrounds with white
space ONLY - do NOT copy its content, characters, or composition, and do
NOT copy its crosshatch shading, fur modelling, gradients, or rendered
background objects; the object-level negatives in this brief override
anything the reference does.

AVOID
watermark, signature, artist logo, extra borders, drop shadows between
panels, photorealistic real people's faces, gore, paragraphs of text,
garbled lettering.
