$imagegen
Use case: illustration-story

ASSET
One square image, 2304x2304. A clean 3x3 grid of nine equal square panels
(each 768x768), separated by thin uniform white gutters about 1% of the
image width. No outer border, no drop shadow between panels, no page edge.

MEDIUM - READ FIRST, THIS OVERRIDES EVERYTHING
Hand-inked brush-pen comic line art. Every outline is a thick India-ink
brush stroke with visible taper - thick where the brush presses, thin
where it lifts - and the occasional deliberately broken or skipped
contour. Interior detail lines (folds, facial features, shelf edges) are
drawn with a much thinner nib. NEVER a smooth even-weight digital vector
outline. Faces and poses are loose and slightly exaggerated, like quick
comic sketches, NOT a polished animation still.
Colour is flat gouache blocks laid under the ink: chalky, low-saturation,
ONE flat colour per area, hard edges, at most 3 flat value steps in the
whole panel. Hair is ONE flat brown shape with maybe one darker flat
lock - no strand detail, no sheen. Skin is ONE flat tone - no blush
gradient, no cheek shading.

OBJECT-LEVEL NEGATIVES (hard constraints - the first pass failed these)
NO soft shading, NO airbrush, NO gradient anywhere (hair, skin, walls,
plates), NO glossy or specular highlights, NO ambient-occlusion shadow,
NO drop shadows, NO rendered volume, NO 3D modelling, NO cel-anime
polish. NO crosshatching, NO hatching, NO stippling, NO pencil texture,
NO paper grain, NO wood-grain lines, NO fabric weave, NO rendered
patterns. Walls are a single flat cream fill. Do not outline every fill.
Backgrounds stay sparse: one or two flat shapes (a window rectangle, an
open cupboard as flat rectangles, an empty shelf) and lots of plain
white/cream space - never a furnished, textured room.

SCENE TO ILLUSTRATE (same scene restaged nine ways)
The mnemonic sentence: "One pea a day, Nia lacks food." A thin young girl
named Nia sits at a nearly bare wooden table. On the table is a single
round green pea on an otherwise empty white plate - her whole meal for
the day. Cupboards and bowls are empty; she looks hungry and weak. Every
panel must show: Nia, the one green pea, the empty white plate, the bare
table, and an empty cupboard or empty bowl.

THE MNEMONIC HOOK ELEMENT
The single green pea. In every panel the pea gets the compositional
emphasis: foreground placement, the boldest, thickest brush contour on
the sheet, and the single most saturated gouache block on the sheet (a
clear mid-green - everything else is muted). It is tiny but must be the
first thing the eye lands on - oversized visual weight, e.g. Nia staring
right at it, pointing, or holding it up.

PANEL 1 IS THE STYLE KEY - RENDER IT FIRST
Panel 1 (top-left): eye-level medium shot. Nia sits square at the bare
table, both hands flat on the wood, staring down at the single green pea
on the white plate. Behind her an empty cupboard drawn as two flat
rectangles with bare shelf lines. Flat cream wall, generous white space.
Thick tapered brush outline on Nia and the plate, thicker still on the
pea; thin nib lines for her face and the shelves; flat chalky fills, no
shading at all. The other eight panels must match panel 1's brush-line
weight, its broken-contour looseness, and its flat no-shading fills
EXACTLY.

THE NINE STAGINGS (row-major; same girl, same pea, same plate, same bare
table, same story beat - only the staging changes)
1. Eye-level medium shot: Nia sits square at the table, hands flat on the
   wood, staring down at the single pea on the white plate. Empty
   cupboard behind.
2. Tight close-up: the single green pea on the white plate fills the
   lower foreground, huge; Nia's hungry face small and simple behind it.
3. Low hero angle looking up: Nia holds the one pea pinched between
   finger and thumb above her head, squinting at it against a pale
   window.
4. Wide establishing shot: the whole bare kitchen - Nia tiny at the far
   table, one pea on the plate, empty open cupboard, empty bowl on the
   floor, huge empty room, lots of white space.
5. Over-the-shoulder from behind Nia: we look past her thin shoulder and
   messy hair down at the plate with its one green pea and nothing else.
6. Top-down flat lay: the round table from directly above - white plate
   dead centre, single green pea on it, an empty bowl and an upturned
   empty cup beside it, Nia's two hands entering from the bottom edge.
7. Side profile, seated: Nia in profile lifts a fork with the single pea
   speared on the tip toward her open mouth, eyes wide, thin frame.
8. Three-quarter view, standing: Nia on tiptoe pulling open a bare
   cupboard with one hand, other hand cupping the single pea at her
   chest, dismayed.
9. After the meal: the empty white plate with a tiny green smear where
   the pea was, fork set down; Nia slumped back in the chair, one hand on
   her stomach, a wall calendar behind marked with a small green dot on
   one day.

TEXT
No panel labels, numbers, captions, watermarks, signatures, or titles.
Diegetic text only: the panel 9 calendar may show a short month name or a
single green dot - short, correctly spelled. No speech bubbles.

INPUT IMAGES
Image 1: style reference for line looseness, line-weight variation, flat
limited low-saturation palette, and sparse flat backgrounds with white
space ONLY - do NOT copy its content, characters, or composition, and do
NOT copy its crosshatch shading, fur modelling, gradients, or rendered
background objects; the object-level negatives in this brief override
anything the reference does.

AVOID
watermark, signature, artist logo, extra borders, drop shadows between
panels, soft shading, gradients, glossy highlights, cel-anime polish,
photorealistic faces, gore, paragraphs of text, garbled lettering.
