# C-013 Pivot words

**Meaning:** The words in the story sentence that carry the sound anchors, one per anchored chunk, in chunk order. A pivot can be a form of its keyword, such as "tried" for "try".
**Example:** trial: "The court tried a dull case, weighing his guilt." Its pivots are tried, dull and guilt.
**Not the same as:** a keyword, which is the anchor word found for a chunk. The pivot is how that keyword appears in the sentence.
