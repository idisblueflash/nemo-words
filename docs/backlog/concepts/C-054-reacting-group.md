# C-054 Reacting group

**Meaning:** A group of onlookers that only reacts to a cause and does no action of its own. The cause is the main subject (C-049), also when it is an object or a person. The group is not the main subject. Seen from the back, toward the cause, the group leads the eye to the cause and hides its faces. A faces part scores the group's faces: no faces seen, or faces small or turned away, score highest; many large front faces score lowest.
**Example:** appeal's story "A peal of bells attracts a curious crowd." The crowd only reacts to the bells, so the bells are the main subject and the crowd is a reacting group. Flash's cell 9, where the crowd is seen from the back, is best.
**Not the same as:** the main subject (C-049), which is the cause here. A reacting group is one kind of onlooker (C-052).
