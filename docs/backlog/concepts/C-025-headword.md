# C-025 Headword

**Meaning:** The word that a card teaches. It is the `Front` of the card. A story must not contain the headword or an inflection of it, and the headword is never its own sound anchor.
**Example:** For the card `concede`, the headword is `concede`. A story that says "conceded" fails the hard gate with `headword: conceded`.
**Not the same as:** a sound anchor, which is a different, known word that only sounds like a part of the headword.
