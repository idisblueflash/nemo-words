# C-049 Axis part

**Meaning:** One thing that the plausible axis or the vivid axis checks, with its own Jev question. Plausible has 3 parts: setting, drawing and story actions. Vivid has 2 parts: scene and onlooker. The axis score is the mean of its parts, as must-see clarity is the mean of the clarity parts. The parts have no weights of their own.
**Example:** overreach cell 1 shows the hanging and the stretch but not the fall. Its story-actions part is 3, because it shows the beginning actions in order, and the reader imagines the fall.
**Not the same as:** an axis weight (C-039). Only the 3 axes have weights.
