# C-001 Story

**Meaning:** One short sentence on the card's Back. It builds the word again from sound anchors (for the sound) and a concrete scene (for the meaning). The word itself, or any form of it, never appears in it.
**Example:** drown: "The king's crown went down with the ship." — a story that anchors /dɹaʊn/ with "down".
**Not the same as:** the backlog item type "story" (US-NNN), and the story arm, which is the group of words in the E02 test that get a story card.
