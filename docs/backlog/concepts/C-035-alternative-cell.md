# C-035 Alternative cell

**Meaning:** A second grid cell offered next to the auto-picked one. It is a wider shot when the picked cell is an extreme close-up that cuts off the setting, or a different action step when the story is a sequence.
**Example:** shattered: the extreme close-up of the screen is picked, and the close-up that shows the phone on the floor is the alternative cell.
**Not the same as:** a comic, which puts 3–4 cells together 2×2 on one card.
