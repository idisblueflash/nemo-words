# C-029 Lapse

**Meaning:** A review where Flash forgets a card that Flash already learned, and so answers "Again". Anki then sets the card's interval back to a short one. The lapse rate is the number of lapses for each card in one arm. E02 uses it as one of two measures of whether a card lasts.
**Example:** Flash sees a story card 30 days after the last review and cannot remember the word. Flash answers "Again", and Anki counts 1 lapse for that card.
**Not the same as:** a miss on a new card that Flash has not learned yet. Anki does not count that as a lapse. Also not the same as the 21-day interval (C-028), which is the other E02 measure.
