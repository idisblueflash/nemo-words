# C-052 Onlooker

**Meaning:** A person or an animal in a picture that doesn't do the story's action but can watch it or react to it. An onlooker that watches or reacts makes the story's feeling stronger. Seen from the back, an onlooker also leads the eye to the action. An onlooker that looks away or does something else draws the eye away from the action. An onlooker is the vivid onlooker part (C-053), not the main subject.
**Example:** in flustered, Flash's cell 6 shows a customer seen from the back while the waiter fumbles the mustard. The customer is an onlooker who makes the waiter's red face stronger. In appeal, the curious crowd is the onlooker, and the bells are the main subject (C-049).
**Not the same as:** the main subject (C-049), which does the action.
