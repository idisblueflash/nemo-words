# C-019 Gloss

**Meaning:** One short clause of plain English that gives the meaning, 60 characters or fewer. It is shown on the card under the story. It has no etymology and no examples.
**Example:** trial: "a formal court hearing, or a test run".
**Not the same as:** the definition from `define`, which is longer and is not shown on a story card. A control card's Back is the definition.
