# C-042 Angle exception

**Meaning:** A story where an unusual camera angle is right: the story is about a view, or it has one main object. In all other stories, the image uses a normal angle at eye level (ADR-0033).
**Example:** A story about looking down from a ridge is about a view, so a high angle from above does not lower its vivid score.
