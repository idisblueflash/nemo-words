# C-017 Kept verdict

**Meaning:** What happened to one auto-picked card after review. `kept` means the story on the card is unchanged. `overridden` means Flash changed or replaced it, even by one typo, or deleted the card. `not-met` means it has no review yet.
**Example:** Flash rewrites wary's story in the Anki app, so `kept-count.js` counts wary as overridden.
