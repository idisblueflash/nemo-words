# C-045 Main subject

**Meaning:** The one must-see item that a story is about. The caption gives its view: front, three-quarter, side, back or from above. The view is one part of must-see clarity: a front or three-quarter view scores highest, a side view in between, and a back view or a view from above lowest.
**Example:** surgeon's story "Power surge! An OR goes dark; she operates by headlamp." has the main subject `surgeon`. In cell 1 she is seen in three-quarter view, in cell 8 from the side, and in cell 5 from above.
**Not the same as:** the angle exception (C-042), which is about the camera angle of the whole story; the main-subject view is about where the main subject's face and body point in one cell.
