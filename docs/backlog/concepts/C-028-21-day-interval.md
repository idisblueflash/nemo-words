# C-028 21-day interval

**Meaning:** A card reaches it when Anki schedules its next review 21 or more days later. Anki then calls the card "mature". E02 uses it as one of two measures of whether a card lasts: more of the cards in one arm reach it than the cards in the other arm.
**Example:** Flash answers "Good" on a story card, and Anki sets the next review to 25 days later. That card now counts as having reached a 21-day interval.
**Not the same as:** a lapse (C-029), which is the other E02 measure. A lapse is a review where Flash forgets a card that Flash already learned. Anki then sets the interval back to a short one.
