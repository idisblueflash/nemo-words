# C-026 Closed chunk

**Meaning:** The stressed chunk of a word, made one consonant longer. When the stressed syllable ends in a short vowel (/ɪ ɛ æ ʌ ʊ/), the chunk also takes the first consonant of the next syllable. The next chunk keeps that consonant too, so two chunks use it.
**Example:** penalty is /ˈpɛ.nəl.ti/. The stressed syllable is /pɛ/, and the closed chunk is /pɛn/, which is exactly "pen". The next chunk is still /nəl/.
**Not the same as:** a syllable, which stays /pɛ/ on the card's IPA line and in the mnemonic log. Also not a rhyme anchor: a closed chunk gets a word with exactly its sound.
