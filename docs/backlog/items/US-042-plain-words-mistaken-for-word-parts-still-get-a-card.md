# US-042 Plain words mistaken for word parts still get a card

As Flash, I want a plain-English word that the classifier calls medical only because part of it matches a word part (e.g. trial, period, premise) to get a story card anyway, so that it isn't stopped on the word-part path.

**Scope (split from US-040):** the same Jev question as US-040, asked for `medical` verdicts too, so a false match is overruled. 6 of the 21 `medical` verdicts on logged words are plain English (trial, period, premise, substance, apprenticeship, trivial).
