# E03 Auto-picked stories and images get better the more Flash corrects them

**Outcome:** When Flash overrides an auto-picked story or image, the app learns from it, so later picks need fewer overrides.
**Success signal:** The rate at which Flash overrides auto-picks goes down across successive batches of 20 words.
**Assumption tested:** #2 (pick quality can improve over time) and #3 (AI drafts can be improved).
**Out of scope:** Re-storying cards based on review failures (E06).
