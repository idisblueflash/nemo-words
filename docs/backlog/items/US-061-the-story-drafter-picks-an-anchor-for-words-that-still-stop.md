# US-061 The story drafter picks an anchor for words that still stop

As Flash, I want a word that stops with "no sound anchor found" to get a short list of words that contain or overlap its sound, from which the story drafter picks one (bulge → bulb, indulge; garage → barrage), so that these words get a card too.

**Scope (split from US-050, 2026-10-13):** investigation-007: 7 of 316 words still stop (breadth, bulge, durable, garage, realms, rigged, scarce). Only "contains" and "overlap" anchors reach all 7, and frequency can't choose among them (a median of 111 candidates for each chunk). A narrower rule, a word with the same sound from the stressed vowel to the end, has a median of 4 candidates and rescues 3 of the 7.
