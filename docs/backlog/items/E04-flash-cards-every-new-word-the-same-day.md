# E04 Flash cards every new word he meets while reading, the same day

**Outcome:** Capturing a word while reading and getting its card is cheap and quick enough that Flash does it for every new word, not a selected few.
**Success signal:** At least 90% of captured words are carded within 24h, with the median cost and time per card recorded.
**Assumption tested:** #4 (one card is cheap and fast enough to make for every new word).
**Out of scope:** A mobile app; capture from sources other than Flash's reading flow.
