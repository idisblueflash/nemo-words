# US-050 Split or shifted chunks as sound anchors

As Flash, I want the anchor search to try words that span or shift a syllable boundary, or contain the chunk's sound (vapor→vape/per, stampede→stamp/peed, bulge→bulb/judge, critique→antique), so that words with no exact or rhyme anchor still get a card.

**Scope (split from US-046, 2026-10-12):** one of the three anchor types in Flash's own cards for no-anchor words; US-046 does the rhyme.
