# US-035 Sequence words get an auto comic

As Flash, I want words whose meaning is a sequence (backfire, overreach) to get an auto-composed 2×2 comic instead of a single image, so that the story's steps show.

**Pick rule (from US-028 refinement):** panels follow the story sentence's actions and their consequence, in order. Where the 4 panels allow, add an opening scene first and an ending scene last.
