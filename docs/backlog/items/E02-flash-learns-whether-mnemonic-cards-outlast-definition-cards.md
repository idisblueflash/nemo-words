# E02 Flash learns whether mnemonic cards outlast definition cards

**Outcome:** Flash has evidence from his own Anki reviews on whether mnemonic+image cards are remembered longer than definition-only cards.
**Success signal:** A matched set of at least 80 mnemonic and 80 definition-only cards, compared on lapse rate and on reaching a 21-day interval, from real Anki review data, with the Wilson interval of each arm's rate (ADR-0036). With 30 cards per arm, only a very large difference is visible: on 30 cards, a 20% lapse rate has a range of 10% – 37%, and 40% has 25% – 58%. Start the control deck early: the data takes weeks to build up.
**Assumption tested:** #1 (sound+meaning+scene story with an image beats a definition card). See investigation-005.
**Out of scope:** A general analytics dashboard; testing the Anki reviewer persona.
